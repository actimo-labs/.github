## Who are we?
Actimo Labs is a group of engineers and scientists attempting to understand protein-protein interactions in ML from the first principles of physics and energy modelling.

We build physics and energy-based models for antibody discovery: epitope mapping, binding affinity prediction, docking, de novo and optimisation design, and high-throughput screening.  Based in Melbourne, Australia.

Visit our website to get in touch with the team: https://www.actimolabs.com

ActiMap, our epitope mapping model, is available for commercial use. It takes antibody sequences and an antigen structure and returns residue-level epitope predictions with per-residue confidence scores.  We also offer binding affinity prediction, docking, binder design and high-throughput screening across large candidate pools.
